# The Baisa Business Brain

The strategy & intelligence layer for The Baisa — a companion to Baisa OS (this repo's
app), which holds the live registers and data.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `baisa-business-brain.html` | The interactive console — open directly in any browser. 12 sections: Command Deck (live festival production radar + editable KPI scorecard), Brand & Model (channel price map), Costing Engine (COGM → per-channel price ladder with GST-band and floor guards), Catalog Intelligence (computed from the 29 live styles), Factory Floor, Quality Gate, Channels & Sales, Brand Engine, Money, Compliance & Schemes, Team & Rituals (Poonam's self-resetting daily run-sheet), and the 30/60/90 install plan. Inputs persist in the browser's local storage. |
| `OPERATING_MANUAL.md` | The same system as plain text — canonical reference for Ashish, Poonam, and AI agents/sessions working on the business. |

Also published as a private Claude artifact for daily use (see the artifact gallery —
"The Baisa Business Brain"). The repo copy is the source of truth; update both together
at the quarterly review.

**How it's used:** Monday 30-minute command review, weekly KPI fill, Cash Friday, and the
costing engine before any new style is cut. Channel commissions and scheme terms are
planning references — verify current terms before commitments.
